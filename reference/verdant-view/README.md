# Verdant View sources

All three supplied PDFs are archived here. All seven pages were read, rendered and inspected, including every embedded image. Repeated banners, logos, old navigation/footer text, print timestamps and admission banners are excluded from the page.

`source-passages.json` was extracted independently from the PDFs with line wrapping removed. Tests compare the rendered content model to this source record. The public copy follows the user's supplied paragraphs: typographic quotation marks replace straight quotes, and the stray space before the full stop in `pots .` is removed. No substantive content is omitted or added.

## Source details and limits

- The Vegetable Garden's second photograph and full paragraph occur on page 2. Both source photographs are included.
- Herbal Garden's paragraph continues on page 2, after the print template inserts a Powered By credit in extraction order. The complete paragraph is joined without that template text.
- The display title uses `Nakshathravanam` as requested. Its paragraph retains `Nakshatravanam`, the original source spelling.
- The Vegetable Garden paragraph says `using organic manure and pesticides` and `sold within the campus`. These words are retained without inferring pesticide-free production, certification or supply to the hostel.
- The older About vision source mentions hostel vegetable supply, a plant count and an end-of-2018 target. Those historical claims remain in About and were not imported into Verdant View.
- Herbal Garden's description is introduced as the school's account of traditional plant knowledge. No treatment advice, additional efficacy claims, species lists or plant counts were added.
- Birth-star plant associations and the ten flowers are not enumerated in the sources, so no catalogue or lookup was invented.
- All four relevant photographs are available. Their limited original resolution and historical material have not been represented as a new verification of current garden conditions.
